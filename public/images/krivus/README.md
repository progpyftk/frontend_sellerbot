### Marca e presença digital

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: premium editorial photograph for Krivus business consulting and digital marketing website hero, landscape 3:2.
Create a sophisticated photorealistic art-directed still life about building a coherent brand and digital presence. A matte midnight navy desk, sculptural folded pale arctic blue paper, carefully arranged thick unbranded business stationery, a small raspberry color sample card, and a slim dark laptop angled three quarters with an elegant abstract editorial website composition on its screen: large colored blocks and a still-life photograph, absolutely no readable text. A phone beside it echoes the same simple visual. Physical objects are grounded and realistic, restrained composition, deliberate breathing room, strong diagonal daylight from the upper left, tactile paper grain, subtle natural shadows. Camera at elevated three-quarter angle, 50mm editorial product photography, crisp materials, no stock-photo corporate clichés.
Palette tied to Krivus: midnight #101e2b, arctic #edf4f7 and #b8cbd6, raspberry #a51e55 as a small deliberate accent. Image should feel like a refined branding studio art direction, luminous objects against dark surroundings.
Constraints: no people, no hands, no readable text or letters, no logos, no watermarks, no platform icons, no graphs or invented performance numbers, no glowing neon, no floating objects. Keep all principal objects inside central 85 percent for responsive cropping.

### Clínicas

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: editorial illustrative photograph for the clinics section of Krivus marketing website, landscape 3:2.
Create a refined, welcoming contemporary Brazilian medical clinic reception, photographed as architecture editorial. A gently curved light stone reception counter on the left, comfortable pale blue upholstered waiting chairs on the right, warm light wood detailing, a single leafy plant, soft indirect daylight through a tall frosted window. Reception wall is pale arctic blue and a small raspberry upholstered accent provides connection to the brand. Calm, credible, human-scale space, modest premium independent clinic rather than luxury hotel. Beautiful material details: subtle stone grain, natural wood, fabric texture, clean realistic architectural lines. Eye-level camera, natural 35mm perspective, soft shadows and balanced contrast, no exaggerated ultra-wide distortion. Compose with the counter and chairs readable even when cropped at mobile width.
Palette: arctic blue #dfe9ef, light #edf4f7, slate #152d43, restrained raspberry #a51e55. Warm natural light balances cool brand colors.
Constraints: no people or patients, no names, no logos, no signage, no readable text, no watermarks, no medical procedures or equipment, no before-and-after, no collage. This is an illustrative setting, not documentation of an actual client.
